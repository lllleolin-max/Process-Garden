# Independent theme directions

用户明确要求主题不是同轮廓换皮。接口共享，形态和叙事各自设计。

- Garden: botanical microcosm, growth, seeds and pollination. Jade, moss, dew and amber light.
- Eldritch final direction: reuse the originally approved blue/cyan eye-core, jewel-like organisms, coiled tentacles and vibrant emerald/violet/amber accents. All nine blue-family images were refined by image generation using the original local images as references, preserving composition and silhouette. The user rejected the grey-green marine redesign as pale and less attractive. Runtime selects `eldritch-blue/`; the older `eldritch/` experiment is superseded.
- Cyberpunk: engineered data infrastructure, triangular drones, cubic vaults, compute cartridges, power canisters, manipulators and fabrication capsules. Mechanical assembly instead of biological growth.
- Crimson Gaze / 猩红凝视: independent black-and-red ocular Cthulhu. Crowned ancient eye, ruby irises, obsidian eyelids, eye-chain serpents, winged eyes and cathedral shrines. Tasks awaken through eyelid stages; ocular tendons retrieve them and the central pupil dilates. Preserve the blue/teal deep-sea family alongside it.

Superseded first-pass outputs are not selected for these redesigned roles. Exact final prompts and source files are recorded in GENERATION.json.
